  1. Anthropic warns small AI models may be months from self-replicating via smart devices

    Anthropic said tests show models under 30B parameters can already do many steps needed to copy themselves onto poorly secured internet-connected devices, though none completed the full chain; stronger models did so in simplified tests that skipped some security steps. It said small models may be months away and autonomous replication could arrive by year-end.
