Summary of the day · Sunday, 2 August 2026

EU AI Act transparency rules take effect; Gatekeeper rises

Rules came into force on both sides of the Atlantic. In the EU, AI Act transparency duties began: chatbots must disclose themselves, deepfakes must be labelled, and the AI Office can now enforce rules on general-purpose models. Rules for high-risk systems were delayed to December 2027. California's AI Transparency Act also took effect, requiring provenance marks and detection tools. Binding rules like these raise Global Coordination, which lifts Gatekeeper and eases Self-Destruction. The EU duties also limit hidden AI manipulation, lowering Surveillance & Control and trimming Benevolent Dictator, which depends on accepted monitoring. Horizon Balance improved to -0.63.

Biggest movers. Gatekeeper +0.6, mostly from the EU AI Act milestone (+0.56), partly offset by fading earlier news (-0.04). Self-Destruction -0.5, from the EU AI Act milestone (-0.40) and California's law (-0.04). Benevolent Dictator -0.2, from the EU AI Act milestone (-0.16).
