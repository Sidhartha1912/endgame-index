Summary of the day · Monday, 2 November 2026

Anthropic filing says Claude may soon hit its self-acceleration threshold

Anthropic's amended IPO filing said Claude may 'imminently' reach the Responsible Scaling Policy threshold for AI-driven acceleration of AI research, and disclosed about $895 billion in compute commitments. That pushed both Capability Velocity and Power Concentration up. Marvell's roughly $115 billion all-stock deal for Lumentum added more to Power Concentration, which rose 0.32 on the day, the largest factor move. Abu Dhabi signed four deals with OpenAI, including machine-readable government rules, lifting AI Autonomy. Meituan's 1.6-trillion-parameter LongCat-2.5, trained on Chinese chips, and Germany's 'KI-Nation 2030' plan added to Capability Velocity, while SUSE's 10% job cuts, citing AI, nudged Shared Prosperity down. Zookeeper, Enslaved God and Benevolent Dictator rose. Horizon Balance fell to -10.07 from -9.98.

  1. Anthropic S-1/A says Claude may 'imminently' hit its RSP threshold for AI-driven acceleration

    Anthropic's amended IPO filing says it may imminently meet its Responsible Scaling Policy threshold for dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI, though no model has yet been designated as crossing it. The filing also discloses about $17B in Q3 revenue and $895.4B in compute commitments.

  2. Meituan launches LongCat-2.5, 1.6T-parameter multimodal agent model trained on Chinese chips

    Meituan released LongCat-2.5, a 1.6-trillion-parameter MoE (about 48B active) for long-horizon agent work, which it says was trained on domestic Chinese accelerators. Meituan claims it beats GPT-5.6-Sol on 20 of 27 shared tests; no independent evaluation yet, and weights are planned but not out.

    Marvell will acquire optical-component maker Lumentum in an all-stock deal valued at about $115B, targeting AI data-center connectivity. Closing is targeted for the first half of 2028.

    Abu Dhabi and OpenAI will convert a set of government policies into machine-readable form so AI can check permit compliance, with officials keeping final decisions. Mubadala, Inception42 and ADNOC also signed deals to adopt OpenAI's frontier models and Frontier agent platform.
