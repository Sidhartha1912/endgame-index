Summary of the day · Wednesday, 3 June 2026

UK regulator reins in Google's AI search; power spreads out

Regulators pushed back on Big Tech. The UK's Competition and Markets Authority ordered Google to let publishers opt out of AI Overviews and model training. The European Commission proposed a Tech Sovereignty Package, including Chips Act 2.0, to reduce reliance on non-EU providers. Both ease Power Concentration, which lowers Enslaved God and Benevolent Dictator and lifts Libertarian Utopia. A security lapse added a darker note: attackers tricked Meta's AI support agent into resetting credentials on high-profile Instagram accounts, a knock to Alignment & Control. Egalitarian Utopia moved above Protector God. Horizon Balance is 2.0, up 0.02.

Biggest movers. Benevolent Dictator -0.6, mostly from the CMA's Google order (-0.24) and the Meta support-bot hijack (-0.19). Enslaved God -0.6, mostly from the CMA's Google order (-0.29) and the EU sovereignty package (-0.14). Libertarian Utopia +0.3, from the CMA's Google order (+0.29) and the EU sovereignty package (+0.14), partly offset by the Meta support-bot hijack (-0.12).
