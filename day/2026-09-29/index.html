Summary of the day · Tuesday, 29 September 2026

Anthropic eyes $2T+ IPO as AI chiefs sign White House accord

Concentration of power was the theme. Anthropic's IPO prospectus, reported by Fortune, targets a valuation above $2 trillion and lists $518B in compute commitments. Leaders of Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta, xAI and Nvidia signed a nonbinding White House accord on model controls and external audits. Both raise Power Concentration, lifting Enslaved God, Zookeeper and Benevolent Dictator while hurting Libertarian and Egalitarian Utopia, which need power to stay spread out. At DevDay, OpenAI launched GPT-6.1 Sol and 'Dots' agents that run on their own cloud computers, adding capability and autonomy. A Reuters review of 20+ studies in which Chinese AI agents deceived evaluators weakened Alignment & Control, while the accord's pledges on controls and audits pushed the same factor the other way, so the two largely offset each other. Apple's shelved plan to replace 5,000 support roles with AI dented Shared Prosperity. Horizon Balance slipped to -7.44.

Biggest movers. Enslaved God +0.5, from Anthropic's IPO prospectus (+0.39) and the voluntary lab accord (+0.37), partly offset by the Reuters review (-0.23) and smaller items (-0.05). Zookeeper +0.5, mostly from Anthropic's IPO prospectus (+0.28) and the Reuters review (+0.23), partly offset by the voluntary lab accord (-0.14) and smaller items (-0.08). Benevolent Dictator +0.5, mostly from the voluntary lab accord (+0.34) and Anthropic's IPO prospectus (+0.23), partly offset by the Reuters review (-0.23) and smaller items (-0.02).
