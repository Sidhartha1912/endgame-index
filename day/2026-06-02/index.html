Summary of the day · Tuesday, 2 June 2026

Trump signs scaled-back AI security order; Protector God rises

After scrapping an earlier version last month, President Trump signed a narrower AI security order. It directs Treasury, the NSA and CISA to build a classified benchmark and a voluntary framework for government access to frontier models up to 30 days before release, with no licensing. Even voluntary testing strengthens Alignment & Control, which lifts Protector God and Gatekeeper and pushes Conquerors down. Microsoft launched its own in-house MAI models to reduce reliance on OpenAI, slightly spreading power. Anthropic extended Project Glasswing's bug-hunting to power, water and healthcare operators. Horizon Balance rose 1.06 to 2.0, its biggest one-day gain of the period so far.

Biggest movers. Protector God +0.7, mostly from the AI security order (+0.69), partly offset by fading earlier news (-0.03). Conquerors -0.7, mostly from the AI security order (-0.69), partly offset by Microsoft's in-house MAI models (+0.04). Libertarian Utopia +0.6, mostly from the AI security order (+0.50) and Microsoft's in-house MAI models (+0.10).
