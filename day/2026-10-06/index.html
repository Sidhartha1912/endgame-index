Summary of the day · Tuesday, 6 October 2026

Mistral and DeepSeek push open, spread-out AI; cheap phones squeezed

Power spread out. Mistral previewed Large 4, a 1-trillion-parameter model it plans to release with open weights, and DeepSeek neared a $12B funding round ahead of a Hong Kong IPO targeted for Q1 2027. Strong challengers and open weights lower Power Concentration, lifting Libertarian Utopia and cutting Benevolent Dictator, 1984 and Enslaved God. The costs of the AI boom also showed: Rest of World reported sub-$100 phone shipments fell nearly 60% as memory went to AI data centres, hurting Shared Prosperity and Egalitarian Utopia. Google signed a 3.6 GW power deal including new nuclear, and Memphis postponed a data-centre moratorium vote amid protests. Horizon Balance rose to -7.69.

Biggest movers. Libertarian Utopia +0.4, mostly from Mistral's open-weight preview (+0.27) and DeepSeek's funding round (+0.11). 1984 -0.3, mostly from Mistral's open-weight preview (-0.24) and DeepSeek's funding round (-0.08), partly offset by fading earlier news (+0.03). Benevolent Dictator -0.3, from Mistral's open-weight preview (-0.22) and DeepSeek's funding round (-0.10).
