Summary of the day · Wednesday, 17 June 2026

Anthropic signs Korea safety pact; G7 hosts AI lab chiefs

A calmer, slightly brighter day. Anthropic opened a Seoul office and signed an agreement with Korea's science ministry covering Korean-language safety evaluations and cyber-threat sharing. Better safety testing strengthens Alignment & Control, which lifts Protector God and Gatekeeper and pushes Conquerors down. Separately, more than 300 security leaders asked the US to reverse its ban on Claude Fable 5. Horizon Balance rose to +2.32.
