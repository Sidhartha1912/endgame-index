Summary of the day · Thursday, 2 July 2026

OpenAI floats 5% US government stake; VC piles into two labs

Power gathered at the top. Fortune reported OpenAI has proposed giving the US government a roughly 5% stake, worth about $42.6 billion, alongside deeper Pentagon ties. Crunchbase data showed record global venture funding in the first half, with OpenAI and Anthropic taking 43% of it. Both raise Power Concentration, which lifts Enslaved God and Benevolent Dictator and, with closer state ties, 1984, while Libertarian Utopia falls. On the safety side, the White House is reportedly negotiating voluntary pre-release standards with labs, including a 30-day review, a small plus for Alignment & Control. New York became the first state to require labels on ads using AI-generated performers. Horizon Balance: +2.03.

Biggest movers. Benevolent Dictator +0.2, mostly from OpenAI's government-stake idea (+0.12) and the record VC concentration (+0.07).
