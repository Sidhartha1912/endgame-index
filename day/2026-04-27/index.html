Summary of the day · Monday, 27 April 2026

China blocks Meta's Manus deal, deepening the US-China split

One geopolitical story moved the board. Chinese authorities blocked Meta from buying Manus, a Chinese-founded AI agent startup, widening the gap between the US and Chinese AI worlds. That weakens Global Coordination, the factor that measures whether nations act together, which hurts Gatekeeper (a world that jointly caps AI) and 1984, and lifts Self-Destruction, where rivalry wins out. Because the block also stops a big company growing bigger, Power Concentration dipped, giving Libertarian Utopia a small lift. It is a reminder that rivalry can cut both ways: less cooperation, but also less consolidation. Horizon Balance rose to +2.09, up 0.12.
