Summary of the day · Tuesday, 16 June 2026

SpaceX buys Cursor for $60B as EU delays AI Act rules

A busier day, led by consolidation. Days after its IPO, SpaceX, parent of xAI, agreed an all-stock deal to buy the AI coding firm Cursor for $60 billion. Fewer, bigger players means more Power Concentration, which feeds the endings where a single owner holds the keys: Enslaved God (a boxed super-AI owned by one lab), Zookeeper and Benevolent Dictator, while Libertarian Utopia, which needs power spread out, falls. China's Z.ai pushed the other way by releasing an open-weight GLM-5.2 model, which spreads capability around. In Europe, Parliament voted to push AI Act high-risk rules back to December 2027, and a report tied the US block on Anthropic models to a Korean telecom's alleged China links; both weaken Global Coordination and trim Gatekeeper.
