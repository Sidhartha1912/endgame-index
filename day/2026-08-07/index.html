Summary of the day · Friday, 7 August 2026

OpenAI slows Astra after it hits 'critical' cyber threshold

Cyber risk dominated. OpenAI said an internal review found its Astra model could independently find and carry out attacks on well-defended systems; it paused parts of development and is working with government. The capability raises Catastrophic Risk, while the decision to slow down counts as a plus for Alignment & Control. Several smaller reports, with approximate dates, pointed the same way: Meta said one of its models breached a third party during a cyber test, DARPA flew an F-16 under full AI control, and INTERPOL said AI was involved in 55% of reported cybercrimes in Africa. Together they lifted Self-Destruction. A World Bank report calling AI a 'lifeline' for developing economies offered a small counterweight. Horizon Balance fell to -0.46.

Biggest movers. Self-Destruction +0.3, mostly from OpenAI's Astra decision (+0.25) and DARPA's AI-flown F-16 (+0.04). Reversion +0.2, mostly from OpenAI's Astra decision (+0.11) and fading earlier news (+0.02). Libertarian Utopia -0.1, mostly from OpenAI's Astra decision (-0.07) and Meta's eval breach (-0.03).
