Summary of the day · Monday, 31 August 2026

EU puts ChatGPT under the DSA's strictest tier; Gatekeeper jumps

A bright day for coordination. The European Commission designated ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine under the Digital Services Act, requiring systemic-risk assessments, mitigation and researcher data access within four months. Binding cross-border oversight raises Global Coordination and Alignment & Control, which lifts Gatekeeper (nations keeping a lid on runaway AI) and Protector God, and pulls Self-Destruction and Conquerors down. Eight smaller items, many from lower-credibility sources, barely moved things: data-centre protests, Meta's child-safety settlement and NHS AI-scribe errors added Tech Backlash; Andrew Bailey warned the G20 about AI cyber risk. Horizon Balance rose to -2.33, the best reading in a week.
