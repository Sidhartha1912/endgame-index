Summary of the day · Thursday, 21 May 2026

Trump scraps AI testing order; Self-Destruction rises

Washington stepped back from AI oversight. President Trump cancelled the signing of an executive order that would have set up voluntary 90-day government testing of frontier models before release, after lobbying from David Sacks, Elon Musk and Mark Zuckerberg. That weakens both Global Coordination and Alignment & Control, which pulls down Gatekeeper (a world that caps AI by cooperating) and lifts Self-Destruction. The Pentagon also added Shield AI's autonomous piloting to the LUCAS attack drone, raising Catastrophic Risk and AI Autonomy, which pushed Self-Destruction and Conquerors up further. Intuit said it will cut about 8% of staff as it shifts to AI agents, a small hit to Shared Prosperity that trims Egalitarian Utopia. Horizon Balance fell 0.69 to 0.7.

Biggest movers. Self-Destruction +1.0, from the autonomous attack drone (+0.55) and the scrapped testing order (+0.47). Gatekeeper -0.6, mostly from the scrapped testing order (-0.68), partly offset by the autonomous attack drone (+0.07). Conquerors +0.5, from the scrapped testing order (+0.28) and the autonomous attack drone (+0.27), partly offset by fading earlier news (-0.04).
