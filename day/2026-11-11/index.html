Summary of the day · Wednesday, 11 November 2026

US and China plan AI incident hotline; neuron backdoor study worries

Treasury Secretary Bessent said the US and China will set up an AI incident hotline and a 'superintelligence' dialogue, and New York opened RAISE Act registration for frontier labs, both small lifts to Global Coordination. They were outweighed by worrying news. Microsoft Research showed that editing fewer than 10 neurons can backdoor an AI agent while it still passes safety tests, pulling Alignment & Control down 0.10. Records showed a PLA-linked university bought Nvidia H200s through a reseller despite the US ban, and Helsing unveiled Clarity, an agentic platform that prepares military targets, so Catastrophic Risk rose 0.08. Anthropic launched its IPO roadshow and Boise passed a data-center moratorium. Self-Destruction and Conquerors rose. Horizon Balance fell to -10.55 from -10.39.

Biggest movers. Self-Destruction +0.1, from the PLA-linked H200 purchase (+0.14) and the backdoor study (+0.05), partly offset by fading earlier news (-0.04) and smaller items (-0.05). Libertarian Utopia -0.1, mostly from the backdoor study (-0.07) and the PLA-linked H200 purchase (-0.05), partly offset by fading earlier news (+0.03).
