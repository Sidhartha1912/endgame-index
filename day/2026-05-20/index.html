Summary of the day · Wednesday, 20 May 2026

OpenAI model disproves an Erdos conjecture; AI-takeover endings jump

The big story: an internal OpenAI reasoning model produced a proof disproving a bound Paul Erdos conjectured in 1946, and outside mathematicians including Tim Gowers and Noga Alon checked it. An AI doing new, verified research is a strong signal for Capability Velocity and AI Autonomy, which lifts every ending where very capable AI acts on its own, bright and dark alike: Descendants, Zookeeper, Protector God and Conquerors. Conquerors got an extra push from Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark, who put 60%+ odds on recursive self-improvement by 2028, a reminder that Alignment & Control may lag. Meta began about 8,000 layoffs in an AI restructuring and leaked audio showed it logging staff activity to train AI, denting Shared Prosperity. Because dark endings rose most, Horizon Balance fell 0.40 to 1.4.

Biggest movers. Descendants +1.7, from the Erdos proof (+1.74), partly offset by fading earlier news (-0.03). Zookeeper +1.7, mostly from the Erdos proof (+1.55) and Jack Clark's self-improvement forecast (+0.10). Conquerors +1.6, mostly from the Erdos proof (+1.45).
