Summary of the day · Monday, 20 July 2026

Pentagon AI war-data contract lifts Self-Destruction

Military AI led the day. Accenture won a contract worth up to $821M to build a Pentagon 'War Data Platform' feeding more than 1,500 military data streams into AI tools, and Shield AI raised $1.5B for autonomous drones as defence-AI funding surged. Both raised Catastrophic Risk, the factor behind Self-Destruction, and gave a small lift to Conquerors, which also gains in a more militarised world. Pulling the other way, Hugging Face used a Chinese open model to analyse a cyberattack after US models refused, a sign that power is not locked up by a few labs; that lowers Enslaved God. A HarrisX poll found 56% of US voters view AI favourably, easing Tech Backlash, and a judge approved Anthropic's $1.5B authors' settlement. Horizon Balance fell to 1.80.

Biggest movers. Self-Destruction +0.3, from the Accenture Pentagon contract (+0.26) and Shield AI's raise (+0.04). Enslaved God -0.1, from Hugging Face's turn to a Chinese model (-0.07) and fading earlier news (-0.05). Benevolent Dictator -0.07, from Hugging Face's turn to a Chinese model (-0.06) and fading earlier news (-0.02).
