Summary of the day · Friday, 24 July 2026

Claude Opus 5 launches as Congress drafts an AI kill switch

Washington reacted to the OpenAI breach. A bipartisan 'AI Kill Switch Act' would let federal authorities shut down AI models that threaten human life or the economy, and a second bill would require security audits. These are proposals, so they counted lightly, but they point to stronger Alignment & Control and less unchecked AI Autonomy, trimming Conquerors and Zookeeper. Anthropic released Claude Opus 5, which soon led several benchmarks, raising Capability Velocity and pushing Reversion down. Over a dozen tech firms, including Nvidia, Microsoft, IBM and Meta, signed a letter opposing bans on open models, a vote for spreading power out that lifts Libertarian Utopia. Horizon Balance improved to -0.40.

Biggest movers. Reversion -0.2, mostly from the Claude Opus 5 launch (-0.19). Protector God +0.2, from the Claude Opus 5 launch (+0.16) and the Kill Switch Act (+0.04), partly offset by fading earlier news (-0.05).
