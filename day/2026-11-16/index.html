Summary of the day · Monday, 16 November 2026

Google sees first autonomous AI exploit attack; US plans AI 'national champions'

Google's threat team reported the first observed AI agent framework turning security patches into working exploits with no human involved, used in a real data theft; it was the day's main mover, raising Catastrophic Risk by 0.44 and AI Autonomy by 0.22. The Trump administration plans to name more than 24 US firms as AI 'national champions', lifting Power Concentration by 0.11 and lowering Global Coordination by 0.05. OpenAI embedded outside evaluators from Apollo, METR, Transluce and Goodfire, raising Alignment & Control by 0.09. Hong Kong switched on AI facial recognition across SmartView CCTV, Alibaba made Qwen4-Max generally available, and Commerce curbed remote access to AI chips. Self-Destruction and Benevolent Dictator rose; Libertarian Utopia fell. Horizon Balance slipped to -11.00 from -10.86.
