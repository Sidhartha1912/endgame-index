Summary of the day · Friday, 13 November 2026

FBI warns of autonomous AI hacking tool used in real intrusions

Autonomy news pushed the index darker. The FBI warned that attackers are using ARTEX, an autonomous AI penetration-testing framework, in real intrusions, raising Catastrophic Risk. Google said its Antigravity agents autonomously formalized the Weil conjectures in Lean, lifting Capability Velocity and AI Autonomy. OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet so agents can make purchases, disbanded its standalone robotics team as GPT-6 Astra runs robots directly, and LangChain raised $300 million for agent tooling. AI Autonomy rose 0.57, the largest factor move of the month so far. Weight forensics suggested DeepSeek V4 and Kimi K3 were trained on export-banned Nvidia chips, lowering Global Coordination. Conquerors, Descendants and Zookeeper all rose sharply. Horizon Balance fell to -10.87 from -10.70.
