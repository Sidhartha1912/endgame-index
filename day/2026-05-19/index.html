Summary of the day · Tuesday, 19 May 2026

Google I/O pushes agents and models forward; Descendants climbs

Google I/O dominated. Google launched Gemini 3.5 Flash and Gemini Omni, which lifts Capability Velocity, and introduced Gemini Spark, an always-on agent that can act across Gmail and even buy things for users within limits. That lifts AI Autonomy, the factor behind endings where AI runs on its own: Descendants, Zookeeper and Benevolent Dictator all rose. Google and Samsung also confirmed Gemini-powered smart glasses for this fall, a small step for Human-AI Merging that helps Descendants. On the safety side, OpenAI, Kakao and ElevenLabs adopted Google's SynthID watermark, a modest boost to Alignment & Control that helps Protector God. KPMG's rollout of Claude to 276,000 staff added to Power Concentration. Horizon Balance edged up 0.03 to 1.8.

Biggest movers. Descendants +0.7, mostly from the Gemini Spark agent launch (+0.32) and the Gemini 3.5 Flash launch (+0.22). Zookeeper +0.6, mostly from the Gemini Spark agent launch (+0.32) and the Gemini 3.5 Flash launch (+0.19), partly offset by the SynthID adoption (-0.05) and smaller items (-0.02). Protector God +0.5, mostly from the Gemini Spark agent launch (+0.27) and the Gemini 3.5 Flash launch (+0.19).
