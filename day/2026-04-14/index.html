Summary of the day · Tuesday, 14 April 2026

Maine passes first statewide data-center pause — Reversion ticks up

A busy but gentle opening day. Maine's legislature passed the first statewide moratorium on large data centers, pausing approvals for sites over 20 MW until October 2027. That is a clear sign of Tech Backlash, the factor behind Reversion, the ending where people turn away from advanced technology. OpenAI released a cyber-tuned model, GPT-5.4-Cyber, but only to vetted defenders and agencies; keeping a risky tool on a short leash slightly strengthens Alignment & Control, nudging Protector God and Gatekeeper up and Conquerors down. Novo Nordisk said its company-wide OpenAI rollout will slow hiring, a small dent in Shared Prosperity that trims Egalitarian Utopia. A German brain-implant firm, CorTec, prepared its third stroke patient. Horizon Balance reads +3.55, up 0.05.

Biggest movers. Reversion +0.5, mostly from Maine's data-center moratorium (+0.48). Egalitarian Utopia -0.07, from Novo Nordisk's hiring warning (-0.10), partly offset by OpenAI's gated cyber-model release (+0.03). Protector God +0.07, from OpenAI's gated cyber-model release (+0.07).
