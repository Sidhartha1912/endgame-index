  1. Google's Gemini 4 Carbon tops AI rankings, goes first to cyber defenders

    Google introduced Gemini 4 Carbon, its new frontier model, saying it scores 59 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro for finding real vulnerabilities. It is rolling out first to selected cyber defenders through Google's Fairwind program while safeguards are tested.

  2. UK Budget confirms AI security package, adding a unit to respond to rogue agents

    The UK Budget confirmed the AI package announced on 24 October, including £120 million more for the AI Security Institute and the AI Biosecurity Lab, plus £40 million for a National Agentic Security Unit to detect, investigate and respond to rogue agentic systems and £140 million for a Sovereign AI Unit.

  3. New York bans AI companions from posing as humans to minors

    Governor Kathy Hochul signed a law barring companies from offering minors AI chatbot features that mimic human relationships, promote sexual content, self-harm or drug use, or urge secrecy from parents, with fines up to $25,000 per violation. It takes effect on 1 January 2027.
