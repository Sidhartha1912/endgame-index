Summary of the day · Wednesday, 22 July 2026

AMD bets $5B on Anthropic as compute race accelerates

A day of huge compute deals. AMD committed up to $5 billion to Anthropic, which will deploy up to 2 gigawatts of AMD GPUs, and OpenAI unveiled 'Project Camellia', a 3.2-gigawatt data-center campus in Georgia. Both raised Capability Velocity, which lifts the endings that need very capable AI: Enslaved God, Descendants and Zookeeper. The AMD deal also concentrates power with a leading lab, adding to Enslaved God and Zookeeper. Faster progress pushes Reversion down. Elsewhere, the White House accused Moonshot of distilling Anthropic's model and evading chip curbs, a small blow to Global Coordination. Mistral signed an Azure deal to grow European compute, and Monday.com cut about 20% of staff in an AI-linked restructuring. Horizon Balance slipped to -0.53.

Biggest movers. Enslaved God +0.5, from the AMD-Anthropic deal (+0.34) and Project Camellia (+0.22), partly offset by Mistral's Azure deal (-0.04) and smaller items (-0.04). Reversion -0.4, from the AMD-Anthropic deal (-0.22) and Project Camellia (-0.22). Descendants +0.4, from the AMD-Anthropic deal (+0.22) and Project Camellia (+0.22), partly offset by fading earlier news (-0.05).
