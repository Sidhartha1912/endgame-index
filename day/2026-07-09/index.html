Summary of the day · Thursday, 9 July 2026

GPT-5.6 goes public with 'High' cyber and bio risk ratings

Agents went mainstream. OpenAI released GPT-5.6 to the public, with ChatGPT Work, an agent that handles whole workflows; Anthropic launched Claude Cowork the same day, and SpaceXAI released Grok 4.5. More capable AI acting on its own raises Capability Velocity and AI Autonomy, the shared ingredients of Conquerors, Zookeeper and Descendants. OpenAI's own system card rated all three GPT-5.6 models 'High' for cyber and bio/chem risk and documented over-agency incidents, a dent in Alignment & Control that adds to Conquerors. Protector God also rose, since it too needs capable, autonomous AI. The New York Times sought sanctions against OpenAI in its copyright case. Horizon Balance: +1.76.
