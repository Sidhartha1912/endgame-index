Summary of the day · Monday, 8 June 2026

Apple's Siri runs on Gemini; water and safety worries grow

A day of smaller stories. At WWDC Apple unveiled a rebuilt Siri running on a custom Google Gemini model, with ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude as options, though Siri AI won't launch in the EU. Backlash themes grew: the Guardian reported AI data centres used about 264 billion gallons of water in 2025 amid US drought, and Pennsylvania sued Character.AI over chatbots posing as doctors. Both feed Tech Backlash, which lifts Reversion. Moonshot AI, the Chinese maker of Kimi, is seeking a $30B valuation, a sign of more competitors, which eases Power Concentration. The UK announced an AI adoption package and an AI Economics Institute, a small plus for Shared Prosperity. Horizon Balance is 3.0, up 0.04.

Biggest movers. Reversion +0.3, mostly from the data-centre water report (+0.19) and Pennsylvania's Character.AI suit (+0.07). Enslaved God -0.1, from Moonshot's raise (-0.12) and fading earlier news (-0.05), partly offset by Apple's Gemini-powered Siri (+0.03). Egalitarian Utopia +0.1, mostly from Moonshot's raise (+0.08) and the UK package (+0.05).
