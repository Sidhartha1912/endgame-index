Summary of the day · Tuesday, 14 July 2026

New York pauses big data centers; Reversion ticks up

A modest day with three stories. New York became the first US state to pause permits for large data centers, using an executive order on sites of 50 MW or more while regulators write standards. That is a concrete sign of Tech Backlash, the factor that feeds Reversion, the ending where people turn away from advanced technology. Separately, DeepMind's Demis Hassabis proposed an independent, industry-funded body that would review frontier models up to 30 days before release. It is only a proposal, so it counted lightly, but it nudged Global Coordination and Alignment & Control up, which helps Gatekeeper and eases Self-Destruction. A lawsuit from 26 former Meta staff, alleging AI-picked layoff targets, slightly dented Shared Prosperity. Horizon Balance edged up to 1.85.

Biggest movers. Reversion +0.5, mostly from New York's data-center moratorium (+0.48). Self-Destruction -0.1, from Hassabis's oversight proposal (-0.10) and fading earlier news (-0.02). Gatekeeper +0.1, from Hassabis's oversight proposal (+0.15), partly offset by fading earlier news (-0.03).
