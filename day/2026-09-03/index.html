Summary of the day · Thursday, 3 September 2026

GPT-6 Astra lands rated 'Critical' for cyber; Nvidia buys Hugging Face

Two big stories. OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra, claiming top computer-use and math scores; it is the first OpenAI model at its 'Critical' cybersecurity threshold. A sharp step in Capability Velocity and AI Autonomy lifts every ending that needs powerful, independent AI: Descendants, Zookeeper, Conquerors and Protector God. Nvidia also agreed to buy Hugging Face, the main open-model hub, for about $12.9 billion, raising Power Concentration and boosting Enslaved God and Benevolent Dictator at Libertarian Utopia's expense. Other releases from Anthropic, Google, Alibaba and Meta added capability. Against the tide, Senators Sanders and Casar introduced a bill to ban superintelligence, and Uber sided with driver unions to slow robotaxis. Horizon Balance fell to -2.86.
