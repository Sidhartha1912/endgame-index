Summary of the day · Wednesday, 7 October 2026

OpenAI releases 700+ AI math results, including Unique Games proof

A capability landmark. OpenAI published hundreds of math manuscripts from an unreleased model, and Scott Aaronson reported the release includes a Lean-verified proof of the Unique Games Conjecture, a major open problem. OpenAI also began rolling GPT-6 out to all ChatGPT users, including free tiers. Both raise Capability Velocity, lifting every ending that needs very capable AI, from Protector God and Gatekeeper to Conquerors and Zookeeper, and pushing 1984 down. Pushback grew too: Finland ordered Google to pause work at two data-centre sites pending environmental review, and Common Sense Media rated ChatGPT for Teens an unacceptable risk, both feeding Tech Backlash. Horizon Balance slipped slightly to -7.73.

Biggest movers. Enslaved God +1.0, from the math results (+1.00) and the GPT-6 rollout (+0.15), partly offset by Finland's halt to Google sites (-0.12) and smaller items (-0.05).
