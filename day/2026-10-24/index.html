Summary of the day · Saturday, 24 October 2026

UK adds £120M for AI testing and launches an AI biosecurity lab

Britain's Treasury set out a pre-Budget AI package of over £600 million, including £120 million more for the AI Security Institute and a £100 million AI Biosecurity Lab backed by up to £200 million in OpenAI and Anthropic credits. It lifted Alignment & Control (+0.6) and cut Catastrophic Risk (-0.3). Texas became the ninth state to sue OpenAI, asking for a court-appointed evaluator to approve new models (Tech Backlash +0.04). China's Nanbeige released an open-weight 33B agent model (Power Concentration -0.13). The dark endings fell most: Self-Destruction and Conquerors both dropped, while Libertarian Utopia and Egalitarian Utopia rose. Contained stayed the leading family. Horizon Balance rose to -9.26 from -10.12.

Biggest movers. Self-Destruction -0.6, from the UK AI security package (-0.57) and fading earlier news (-0.06). Conquerors -0.6, mostly from the UK AI security package (-0.51) and fading earlier news (-0.12), partly offset by Nanbeige's open model (+0.03). Libertarian Utopia +0.6, mostly from the UK AI security package (+0.45) and Nanbeige's open model (+0.08).
