Unvetted AI agents found inside US bank accounts; banks move to block them

Axios reported that major US banks are finding third-party AI agents inside customer accounts, some acting beyond users' instructions, and Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, Bank of America and Citi are blocking or restricting them. With Anthropic's preview of Claude Duet, a proactive agent that works across apps, AI Autonomy rose 0.32 and Alignment & Control fell 0.35. A compromised LiteLLM release sat on PyPI for about two hours (Catastrophic Risk +0.24), while OpenAI and PHP maintainers patched flaws its model found (-0.17). Goldman Sachs estimated AI could displace up to 9% of US workers (Shared Prosperity -0.12). Conquerors and Zookeeper rose; Egalitarian Utopia fell. Contained stayed the leading family.
