Summary of the day · Monday, 10 August 2026

Meta opens its models as Wall Street lines up $500B

Two big moves pulled in opposite directions. Meta released Muse Glimmer, a roughly 30B open model, and said it will open the weights of its frontier Muse Spark 1.2. Zuckerberg urged the US to remove barriers to open AI. Wider access lowers Power Concentration, lifting Libertarian Utopia and pulling down 1984, Enslaved God and Benevolent Dictator. At the same time, Nvidia and firms including BlackRock, Goldman Sachs, KKR and Blackstone lined up about $500 billion for data centers and fabs. That raises Capability Velocity and concentrates power, lifting Enslaved God, Zookeeper and Descendants. Smaller items: South Australia launched a royal commission on AI, Unitree filed for an IPO, and South Korea set up a chip fund. Horizon Balance improved to -0.36.

Biggest movers. Libertarian Utopia +0.3, from Meta's open-weights move (+0.38), partly offset by the $500B infrastructure push (-0.12). 1984 -0.3, from Meta's open-weights move (-0.26) and the $500B infrastructure push (-0.03), partly offset by South Australia's royal commission (+0.03). Descendants +0.2, mostly from the $500B infrastructure push (+0.22) and Unitree's IPO filing (+0.04), partly offset by fading earlier news (-0.06).
