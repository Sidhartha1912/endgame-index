Summary of the day · Monday, 12 October 2026

Only 3.6% of Chinese model releases published safety tests

A SemiAnalysis study reported by the FT found Chinese developers published safety evaluations for just 3.6% of 857 model releases, arguing Beijing will not 'pace the frontier'. That weakens both Alignment & Control and Global Coordination, lifting Conquerors and Zookeeper. Capability kept rising too: Meta released Muse Wonder, its first frontier-class model, and BloombergNEF projected China will spend $3.9T through 2049 moving power to AI data centres. Police use of Graylark's Raven, which can locate a home from one interior photo, added to Surveillance & Control. Horizon Balance fell to -8.13.

Biggest movers. Conquerors +0.3, mostly from the safety-test study (+0.17) and Meta's Muse Wonder (+0.14), partly offset by fading earlier news (-0.09). Zookeeper +0.3, mostly from Meta's Muse Wonder (+0.14) and the safety-test study (+0.12), partly offset by fading earlier news (-0.08). Reversion -0.2, from Meta's Muse Wonder (-0.17) and China's grid spending (-0.12), partly offset by fading earlier news (+0.04).
