Summary of the day · Friday, 12 June 2026

US forces global shutdown of Fable 5; Gatekeeper slumps

Days after launch, the US government issued an emergency export-control directive barring foreign nationals from using Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 over a reported jailbreak. Anthropic disabled both models for all customers worldwide and disputed the basis. A unilateral, sudden clampdown hurts Global Coordination, which drags down Gatekeeper and lifts Self-Destruction; pulling frontier models also slows Capability Velocity, which raises Reversion. The same day, SpaceX, which absorbed xAI, listed on Nasdaq at about $1.75T in the largest IPO ever, a jump in Power Concentration that weighs on Libertarian Utopia. Security worries added up: CISA cut federal patch windows to three days because AI speeds up attacks, and researchers showed 'agentjacking' of coding agents. Horizon Balance fell 0.30 to 2.5.

Biggest movers. Gatekeeper -1.1, mostly from the Fable 5 shutdown directive (-1.00), partly offset by the humanoid-robot IPO race (+0.03). Self-Destruction +0.6, mostly from the Fable 5 shutdown directive (+0.52) and CISA's patch order (+0.11), partly offset by Japan's cyber standards (-0.07). Reversion +0.4, from the Fable 5 shutdown directive (+0.40) and CISA's patch order (+0.04).
