Summary of the day · Thursday, 23 April 2026

GPT-5.5 lands as Meta cuts 8,000 jobs for AI

A heavy news day. OpenAI released GPT-5.5, an agent-focused model that it rates High risk for biological and cyber capability. That raises both Capability Velocity and AI Autonomy, lifting endings that need powerful, independent AI: Descendants, Zookeeper, Protector God and Conquerors. Meta confirmed about 8,000 layoffs, roughly 10%, starting May 20 to fund its AI build-out. That cuts into Shared Prosperity and pulls Egalitarian Utopia down. Tennessee's new law declaring that AI cannot be a legal person draws a firmer line between humans and machines, weakening Human–AI Merging and trimming Descendants. Tencent's open Hy3 model was a small counterweight to power concentration. Horizon Balance slipped to +1.78, down 0.17, and two endings swapped places.

Biggest movers. Egalitarian Utopia -0.5, from Meta's 8,000 layoffs (-0.56), partly offset by Tencent's open Hy3 preview (+0.03). Protector God +0.5, from the GPT-5.5 release (+0.52). Zookeeper +0.5, from the GPT-5.5 release (+0.56), partly offset by Tencent's open Hy3 preview (-0.03).
