Summary of the day · Wednesday, 19 August 2026

US weighs forcing nations to pick its AI bloc or China's

A busy day with eight stories; the biggest was geopolitical. Reuters reported Washington may warn dozens of countries that joining Beijing's AI framework would shut them out of a US-led coalition.
