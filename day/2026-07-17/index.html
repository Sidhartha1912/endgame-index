Summary of the day · Friday, 17 July 2026

Xi launches a global AI cooperation body; Gatekeeper rises

The day's big story came from Shanghai. In his first World AI Conference keynote, Xi Jinping announced a World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization, reportedly with 29 founding countries, while criticising US export curbs. A new multi-country body is a real step for Global Coordination. That matters for Gatekeeper, the ending where nations work together to keep superintelligence in check, and it eases Self-Destruction, which feeds on a breakdown in cooperation. It also lifted 1984, because coordinated state control can cut both ways. Two smaller items: Tata set a mid-2028 start for India's first chip fab at Dholera, a slight spread of power away from the leaders, and China claimed its first commercial brain-chip implant, an unverified nudge to Human-AI Merging. Horizon Balance rose to 1.90.
