Summary of the day · Monday, 1 June 2026

Anthropic files for IPO as Florida sues OpenAI

Two big themes. Anthropic confidentially filed a draft S-1 for an IPO after a $65B round at $965B post-money, with reported run-rate revenue of about $47B. That is a big step for Power Concentration, lifting Enslaved God and Benevolent Dictator and lowering Libertarian Utopia. Meanwhile Florida filed the first state lawsuit against OpenAI, alleging ChatGPT concealed risks and contributed to harm, and seeking personal liability for Sam Altman. That feeds Tech Backlash, lifting Reversion, and counts as pressure for safer AI. Pushing the other way, Nvidia's RTX Spark chip for running large models locally and Canada's national AI sovereignty plan both spread power out. Horizon Balance is 0.9, up 0.04.

Biggest movers. Reversion +0.4, mostly from the Florida lawsuit (+0.38).
