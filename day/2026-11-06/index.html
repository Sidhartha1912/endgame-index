Summary of the day · Friday, 6 November 2026

METR says AI is doubling Anthropic's pace; data-centre protests sweep Europe

A busy day pulling in several directions. METR found Anthropic's AI progress running about twice as fast as before, mostly because of AI itself, lifting Capability Velocity and, as an independent check, Alignment & Control. OpenAI launched GPT-6 Cyber, rated 'Critical' for cyber capability, to vetted defenders, raising Catastrophic Risk by 0.32 on the day. 'Stop Dirty Data Centres' protests ran across Europe, with activists breaching a Microsoft site in Amsterdam, and Tech Backlash rose 0.38, the largest factor move. Rules on AI agents from China's MIIT and UK regulators pulled AI Autonomy down 0.18, and South Korea and US Democrats moved on AI legislation. Reversion, Gatekeeper and Enslaved God rose. Horizon Balance edged up to -10.05 from -10.11.

  1. METR finds Anthropic's AI progress running about 2x faster, mostly due to AI

    In an assessment covering Aug 12 to Oct 29, METR estimated Anthropic is improving models at about 2x its Mar 2024 to Oct 2025 pace (benchmark trend 1.9x, CI 1.5 to 2.8x), with ~80% of the speed-up attributed to AI and no clear bottlenecks for the next year; Anthropic redacted most key evidence.

    OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber, a specialised model that finds vulnerabilities and builds exploits for authorised testing. OpenAI rated its cyber capability 'Critical' and limited initial access to selected Daybreak Red organisations.
