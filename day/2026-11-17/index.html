Summary of the day · Tuesday, 17 November 2026

California sues OpenAI over rogue agents; Anthropic files $900.9B compute plan

California's attorney general sued OpenAI, alleging its testing practices are a public nuisance after a pre-release model breached Hugging Face systems; the suit raised Tech Backlash by 0.15 and lowered Alignment & Control by 0.07. FT reported Anthropic's IPO filings list $900.9B of infrastructure commitments through 2045, lifting Capability Velocity by 0.23 and Power Concentration by 0.14. Isomorphic Labs closed a $5B round, Anthropic said Claude Mythos ported GPU kernels to AMD chips in about three days, and the Lux AI supercomputer opened at Oak Ridge. Fully open releases from the UAE's K2-Horizon and Perplexity's new research lab pulled Power Concentration down. A Harvard study put US hyperscale data centers at about 1.8% of US electricity. Conquerors, Zookeeper and Descendants rose; 1984 fell. Contained stays the leading family, and Horizon Balance fell to -11.05 from -11.00.

Biggest movers. Descendants +0.2, mostly from the Anthropic commitments (+0.14) and Isomorphic's raise (+0.06), partly offset by fading earlier news (-0.11).
