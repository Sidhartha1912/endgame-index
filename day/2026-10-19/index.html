Summary of the day · Monday, 19 October 2026

Anthropic pledges 5% of its shares to a fund for AI's economic impact

Anthropic announced it will put 5% of its shares into a new Public Benefit Fund for AI's economic and labour impacts, subject to stockholder approval, which lifted Shared Prosperity by 0.70. The same day its own study showed entry-level workers fell from 27% to 9% of its technical hires, cutting Shared Prosperity by 0.30. Meta described its gigawatt-scale Prometheus supercluster (Capability Velocity +0.17) and DeepSeek released the open-weight V4.1-Flash-1017 update. Egalitarian Utopia rose 0.34, the day's only notable move. Contained stayed the leading family. Horizon Balance rose to -8.04 from -8.17.

Biggest movers. Egalitarian Utopia +0.3, mostly from Anthropic's Public Benefit Fund (+0.49) and fading earlier news (+0.04), partly offset by Anthropic's entry-level hiring study (-0.21). Reversion -0.07, from Meta's Prometheus cluster (-0.10) and DeepSeek's V4.1-Flash-1017 (-0.02), partly offset by fading earlier news (+0.05).
