Summary of the day · Friday, 1 May 2026

Pentagon signs seven AI firms for war networks — Self-Destruction jumps

One defence story made the day. The US Defense Department signed agreements with Google, OpenAI, Nvidia, Microsoft, Amazon, SpaceX and Reflection to run AI on classified networks, aiming for an 'AI-first fighting force'. Anthropic was left out and is still designated a supply-chain risk. Militarised AI raises Catastrophic Risk and also AI Autonomy, since AI takes on more operational roles. That lifts Self-Destruction, where conflict ends the story, and Conquerors, where capable AI slips out of control in a militarised world. Peaceful endings like Libertarian Utopia and Egalitarian Utopia lost ground. Horizon Balance fell to +1.26, down 0.45, and two endings swapped places.

Biggest movers. Self-Destruction +0.9, from the Pentagon's classified-network AI deals (+0.87). Conquerors +0.4, mostly from the Pentagon's classified-network AI deals (+0.44). Libertarian Utopia -0.4, from the Pentagon's classified-network AI deals (-0.40).
