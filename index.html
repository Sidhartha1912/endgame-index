Today’s reading · updated Friday, 20 November 2026

EU Commission confirms AI-assisted cyberattack; House reveals 879,000 China chip approvals

Two security stories led the day. The European Commission confirmed that attackers used an AI tool in a cyber campaign that took its payroll, medical reimbursement and translation systems offline for over 10 days, raising Catastrophic Risk by 0.64. House Foreign Affairs Chair Brian Mast released licence data showing Commerce approved more than 879,000 Nvidia and AMD AI chips for Chinese firms, some of them military-linked, between December and May. That added to Capability Velocity (+0.50) and lowered Power Concentration (-0.32). Smaller steps: California's attorney general subpoenaed Nvidia over possible China access to its chips, Signal switched on default blocks against AI assistants reading chats, and over 540 Google workers petitioned for AI-free Search in schools. Self-Destruction rose most, followed by Conquerors, while 1984 fell. Successors still leads the families. The Horizon Balance fell to -12.18 from -11.96.

What is the Endgame Index?

The Endgame Index reads each day’s AI news and scores how strongly it points toward each of the 12 long-term futures for humanity and AI described by MIT physicist Max Tegmark in his 2017 book Life 3.0, from Libertarian Utopia to Self-Destruction. Every event is rated with a published rubric and linked to its sources, and the scores are recomputed every night at 11:59 PM IST. How it’s scored · About · Download the data.
