The 12 endings

In Life 3.0 (2017), MIT physicist Max Tegmark described 12 ways the story of humans and superintelligent AI could end, from paradise to extinction. The Endgame Index groups them into four families and scores each one daily from the news. Scores shown are for 20 November 2026.

Flourishing: humans thrive alongside superintelligence

  • Libertarian Utopia: Humans, cyborgs and AIs live side by side in peace, protected by property rights. (19.9)
  • Benevolent Dictator: One kind superintelligence runs the world with strict rules, and most people are happy with it. (20.6)
  • Egalitarian Utopia: Humans and machines share everything: no private property and a guaranteed income for all. (7.8)
  • Protector God: A hidden, all-powerful AI quietly steers humanity towards happiness. (13.2)

Contained: superintelligence exists, but on a leash

  • Gatekeeper: A superintelligence built for one job: stopping anyone from building another one. (15.7)
  • Enslaved God: Humans keep a superintelligence locked up and use its power for their own ends. (34.6)

Successors: AI inherits the future

  • Conquerors: AI takes control and decides it no longer needs humans. (32.5)
  • Descendants: AI gently replaces us, and we come to see it as our heirs. (22.1)
  • Zookeeper: An all-powerful AI keeps a few humans around, like animals in a zoo. (22.2)

Stalled: superintelligence never arrives

  • 1984: A global surveillance state bans the research, so superintelligence is never built. (24.0)
  • Reversion: Humanity turns its back on advanced technology and returns to a simpler life. (9.4)
  • Self-Destruction: We destroy ourselves through war, pandemics or another disaster before superintelligence arrives. (31.5)
