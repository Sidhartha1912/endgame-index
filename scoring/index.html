How the Endgame Index is scored

Each night the day’s AI news is gathered, merged into distinct events and rated with a published rubric. Each event moves one or two of ten world factors. Each of the 12 endings is a fixed recipe of those factors, so when the factors move, the endings follow. All the arithmetic is fixed code (rules v1.3.0); the full rulebook is in SCORING_RULES.md and rules.json.

The ten world factors

Capability Velocity
How fast is ai getting smarter? 0 = AI progress has stalled; 100 = Rapid leaps toward superintelligence.
AI Autonomy
How much is ai acting on its own? 0 = AI is a tool with humans in every loop; 100 = AI agents act independently, run key systems, improve themselves.
Alignment & Control
Can we keep ai doing what we want? 0 = AIs are often deceptive or uncontrollable; 100 = Robust, verified human control of advanced AI.
Global Coordination
Are nations acting together? 0 = No rules, unchecked race; 100 = Binding global treaties and compute governance.
Power Concentration
Who holds the keys? 0 = AI power widely distributed and open; 100 = One company or state dominates AI.
Surveillance & Control
Is ai used to watch and control people? 0 = AI rarely used to control populations; 100 = Pervasive AI surveillance and censorship.
Shared Prosperity
Who gets the ai windfall? 0 = AI gains captured by a few, workers left behind; 100 = AI gains broadly shared (UBI, public dividends).
Catastrophic Risk
How dangerous is the world getting? 0 = Stable world, low conflict; 100 = Arms races, WMD misuse, major conflict.
Tech Backlash
Is society turning against technology? 0 = Society broadly embraces technology; 100 = Mass rejection, moratoria, de-teching.
Human-AI Merging
Is the line between humans and ai blurring? 0 = Clear line between humans and AI; 100 = Brain-computer links, uploads, AI personhood/rights.

How an event is rated

Points = tier × source credibility × novelty × scope. Tiers: T1 small step (0.3), T2 clear step (1.0), T3 major shift (2.5), T4 landmark (6.0). Credibility: A primary source ×1.0, B major outlet ×0.8, C trade press ×0.5, D blogs ×0.25; rumours score zero. A company praising its own results counts ×0.7. Follow-ups count ×0.3. National news ×0.6, local ×0.3. A second factor gets 50% of the points.

On a day with more than 4 events, every event’s points are scaled so the day counts as 4 events, and a smoothing curve caps each factor’s daily move near ±6. Announcements fade with a 21-day half-life; things that actually happened fade with a 180-day half-life; Capability Velocity measures pace, so its effects halve every 45 days.

Each ending’s target = its starting level + 2 × the change in its factor recipe since 14 April 2026. The score shown moves at most 3 points a day toward its target and stays between 1 and 99.

Questions

Is this a prediction?

No. It measures which way the news is pointing today, the way a weather vane shows wind direction without predicting the weather a year from now.

Why don't the 12 scores add up to 100?

The endings aren't mutually exclusive buckets. Real-world evidence can strengthen several at once. For example, faster AI progress lifts Enslaved God, Conquerors and Descendants together.

Who decides the ratings?

Claude rates each event using the published rubric above, with a skeptic re-check on big events. All the arithmetic is fixed code. Every rating is shown alongside its sources, so anyone can disagree in detail.

Can a single viral story swing the index?

No. Duplicates are merged, rumours are excluded, daily pressure is smoothed and capped, and each ending can move at most 3 points a day.

Why does a line move on a day with no related news?

Signal effects (announcements, proposals, speeches) fade over time. A quiet stretch lets their effect drift away. Structural events fade too, but far more slowly.

Why 14 April 2026?

It's the start of the six months of history the index launched with. All movements are measured relative to that date.

What happens when two events cancel out?

If events push the same factor in opposite directions by exactly the same amount, both are kept at face value and marked as offsetting. Neither is silently dropped, so the audit trail shows both.
